# Farm Runners Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Farm Runners is a game for players of all ages, published by Skynet Studio ("we"). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. We wrote it so that both parents and players can read it.

## The short version

- You don't need to give us your name, email or any other personal detail to play.
- We treat every player as a child: the game shows only non-personalised ads, from ad networks certified for children's apps, and never uses your device's advertising ID.
- Gameplay statistics are off unless you turn them on in Options.
- You can delete your data at any time from Options → Delete my data.

## What the game stores and why

**Your game progress.** Your coins, high score, animals, power-ups, missions and the record of your purchases are saved on your device. They are also backed up to Unity Gaming Services (Unity Cloud Save), so you keep them if you reinstall or change phones. To do this the game creates an anonymous player account with Unity; it has no name or email in it. If you choose to sign in with Google Play Games or Apple Game Center, that account is linked so your progress follows you.

**Leaderboards and achievements.** If you use Google Play Games or Game Center, your scores and achievements are sent to Google or Apple under your account there, and their own privacy policies apply.

**Purchases.** Purchases are made through Google Play or the App Store. We never see your payment details. The game keeps a purchase reference so it can give you what you bought exactly once and restore "No Ads" on a new device.

**Ads.** The game shows ads through Unity LevelPlay, with Unity Ads serving them. Every ad request is marked as coming from a child (COPPA and Google Play Families settings), so the ads are not personalised and your advertising ID is not used. To show ads, prevent fraud and count views, the ad provider receives technical information such as your device type, operating system, app version, an app-specific device identifier and an approximate location (country or region) derived from your IP address. It also sees how you interact with the ads. If you buy "No Ads", the game stops showing interstitial ads; videos you choose to watch for a reward still use the ad provider.

**Gameplay statistics (only if you turn them on).** With Options → Share anonymous play data switched on, the game sends Unity Analytics short reports at the end of a run, such as the animal used, the score and coins collected, and missions finished. Unity Analytics also receives device and diagnostic information (device model, operating system, crash reports and an approximate location). The switch is off when you install the game.

**Sharing your score.** The share card is made on your device and goes only where you choose to send it.

We do not sell your information, and we do not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos, precise location or microphone.

## Children

Farm Runners is suitable for children and is designed to comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU and UK GDPR, and Google Play's Families policy. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children beyond what is described above, which is used only to run the game, keep progress, show non-personalised ads and prevent fraud. A parent can delete a child's data at any time from Options → Delete my data, or ask us to by email.

## Your choices and rights

- **Statistics:** turn Options → Share anonymous play data on or off at any time.
- **Delete your data:** Options → Delete my data deletes your cloud backup, your anonymous Unity account and your gameplay statistics. The game on your device keeps working with your local progress, and the cloud backup stays off until you turn it back on in Options. Uninstalling the game removes the local copy.
- **Ads:** buy "No Ads" to remove interstitial ads.
- Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your information, or to object to how it is used. Email us and we will reply within 30 days.

## Service providers

- Unity Technologies: game services, cloud save, analytics, ad mediation and ads. https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy
- Google: Google Play, Google Play Games. https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Apple: App Store, Game Center. https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

These companies may process information in countries other than yours, under their own safeguards.

## How long we keep it

Your cloud save is kept until you delete it from Options. Unity keeps analytics and ad data for the periods set out in its privacy policy.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date at the top. Important changes are also announced in the game's store listing.
